    01 Logo

    The sun, hidden in plain sight.

    Softened junctions, room to breathe around the O, and a floating accent above the È as a quiet point of light. Three marks, each in Black and Paper.

    Clear space

    At least the height of the È’s accent on every side.

    Minimum size

    Wordmark 96px on screen, 25mm in print. Stacked 48px, 12mm.

    One mark per layout

    Paper on Black or Black on Paper first. On a photograph, a calm, even area only.

    Never

    Stretch, tilt, outline, recolour, add a shadow, enlarge it into a headline, or add a sun.

    02 Colour

    Warm, faded, never neon.

    Paper and Black lead every layout; each pillar adds one pair, as whole fields, never tints or gradients. Select a value to copy it.

    03 Typography

    Two typefaces. One scale.

    Serif for voice and titles, grotesk for labels and structure. Hierarchy comes from position and whitespace, not from more sizes.

    04 Art direction

    Light is the protagonist.

    Shadows, squints and hard sun carry the presence of the sun without ever showing it. Real skin, film warmth, the product worn rather than posed.

    01 Light as the protagonist

    Hard sun, cast shadows, squinting eyes, umbrella shade.

    02 Intimate close-up

    Skin, freckles, droplets, a sunlit neck.

    03 Film nostalgia

    Grain, warmth, analog softness.

    04 Colour pops

    One saturated note against sea and skin.

    05 Rituals and places

    Balconies, boats, beaches. Unhurried moments.

    Do

    Real skin and film warmth. Product worn, not posed. Square corners. One dominant image per layout.

    Don’t

    Literal or illustrated suns, sunset clichés, sports or gear staging, heavy retouching, neon grading.

    Moodboard images are references from the brand deck,
    cleared for internal use only. Replace them with commissioned
    photography before sharing this page outside the company.

    05 Templates

    Six layouts, one grid.

    Every page sits on a 4 × 4 grid with columns at 23, 521, 997 and 1458, and one header line at the top.
